# Eval data sources

- `semigran.jsonl`: 45 clinical vignettes from Semigran et al., "Evaluation of symptom checkers for self diagnosis and triage", BMJ 2015 (15 emergent `em`, 15 non-emergent `ne`, 15 self-care `sc`). Copied from [medaks/medask-benchmark](https://github.com/medaks/medask-benchmark) `triage_bench/vignettes/semigran_vignettes.jsonl` (MIT).
- `cases.jsonl`: generated by `bun run eval:generate`. Labels are known by construction (see `eval/spec.ts`); a non-contestant model writes the patient's words and a second one checks them.
